What this can and can't tell you

Six live LTA cameras — three at each checkpoint — get read every five minutes, and rated for how stopped the traffic looks in each direction.

It can't tell you a waiting time. A photograph shows how heavy a queue looks, not how fast it's clearing. Every minute figure here is estimated driving time — never time spent queueing.

Driving figures come from straight-line distance at an assumed 60 km/h, so they run short of real road distance. Public holidays aren't modelled, and they're some of the worst days to cross.
